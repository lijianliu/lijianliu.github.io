C – Line of best fit • 10 questions

💡 Plug the x-value into the equation. Multiply first, then add b.

📚 Key words

Prediction
Use y = mx + b: substitute x, then compute y.
Interpolation
Predicting inside the data range: usually reliable.
Extrapolation
Predicting far outside the data: risky!
Order of operations
Multiply m·x before adding b.

C2-01
A line of best fit for car age in years (x) vs. miles per gallon (y) is y = −0.7x + 35.9. Predict y when x = 7.
✔A.31
✘B.−4.9
✘C.42.2
✘D.−40.8
💡 y = −0.7(7) + 35.9 = −4.9 + 35.9 = 31 mpg.
