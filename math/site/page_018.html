18. Slope from a graph (rate of change)

D – Rates, proportions & slope • 10 questions

💡 Pick two points ON the grid. slope = rise ÷ run = (change in y) ÷ (change in x).

📚 Key words

Rise
Vertical change (up +, down −).
Run
Horizontal change (to the right).
Rate of change
Slope with units, like $30 per month.
Starting amount
Where the line starts on the y-axis (x = 0).
