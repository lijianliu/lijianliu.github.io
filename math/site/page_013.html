13. What does the slope mean?

C – Line of best fit • 10 questions
Topics← Prev13 / 30Next →
💡 Slope sentence: "For each additional 1 [x-unit], the [y] goes up/down by [m] [y-units]."

📚 Key words

Slope = rate of change
How much y changes when x goes up by 1.
Positive slope
y increases as x increases.
Negative slope
y decreases as x increases.
Units
Slope units are "y-units per x-unit" (e.g. mpg per year).

C3-01
x = the number of months, y = dollars saved. Line: y = 30x + 50. What does the slope mean?
✔A.For each 1 month increase, dollars saved increases by 30 dollars.
✘B.When the number of months is 0, dollars saved is 50.
✘C.For each 1 month increase, dollars saved decreases by 30 dollars.
✘D.For each 1 dollars increase, the number of months increases by 30.
💡 The slope is 30: the change in y for each +1 in x.
