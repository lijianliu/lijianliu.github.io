E – Solving equations (Checkpoint 7) • 10 questions

💡 Move x-terms to one side and numbers to the other (do the same thing to both sides), then divide.

📚 Key words

Balance
Whatever you do to one side, do to the other.
Collect like terms
Gather x-terms together and numbers together.
Tip
Move the SMALLER x-term to avoid negatives.
Check
Both sides should give the same number.
