30. Systems word problems: when are they equal?

F – Systems of equations • 10 questions
Topics← Prev30 / 30Next →
💡 Write y = start + rate·x for each person/plan. Set them equal and solve: that’s when they match.

📚 Key words

Start (b)
The amount at time 0.
Rate (m)
The change per month/day/hour.
Equal Values
start₁ + rate₁·x = start₂ + rate₂·x
Meaning of the solution
x = when they are equal, y = the amount they both have then.

✏️ From the homework

Jeffrey has $100 and saves $20/month; Liz has $40 and saves $30/month: 100 + 20x = 40 + 30x → x = 6 months, both have $220.
