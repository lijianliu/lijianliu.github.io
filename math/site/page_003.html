3. Relative frequency (percent of a row)

💡 Relative frequency = part ÷ row total × 100. Divide by the total of THAT row, not the grand total!

📚 Key words

Relative frequency
A count turned into a percent (or fraction) of a total.
Row relative frequency
Divide each cell by its row total. Each row then adds up to 100%.
Percent
part ÷ whole × 100
Rounding
3.8709...% to the nearest hundredth is 3.87%.
