17. Unit rates & better buys

D – Rates, proportions & slope • 10 questions

💡 Unit rate = divide to get the amount for ONE. Lower price per item = better buy.

📚 Key words

Unit rate
A rate with 1 in the denominator: $2.50 per notebook.
Unit price
Cost ÷ number of items.
Better buy
The option with the lower unit price.
Speed
distance ÷ time: a unit rate.

✏️ From the homework

12 feet in 10 hops → 12 ÷ 10 = 1.2 feet per hop.
