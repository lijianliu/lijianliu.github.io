8. Finding outliers on a scatterplot

B – Scatterplots & graphs • 10 questions
Topics← Prev8 / 30Next →
💡 An outlier sits far away from the trend of the other points. Name it with an ordered pair (x, y): x first!

📚 Key words

Outlier
A data point far from the overall pattern.
Ordered pair
(x, y): go across to x, then up to y.
Effect
Outliers can pull a line of best fit away from the rest of the data.
