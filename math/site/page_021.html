E – Solving equations (Checkpoint 7) • 10 questions

💡 Undo in reverse order: first add/subtract, then divide. Tip: multiply by 10 or 100 to get rid of decimals.

📚 Key words

Inverse operations
Addition ↔ subtraction, multiplication ↔ division.
Isolate x
Get x alone on one side.
Clear decimals
Multiply every term by 10 (one decimal place) or 100 (two places).
Check
Plug your answer back in.

✏️ From the homework

2.5x + 1.5 = 14 → 2.5x = 12.5 → x = 5.
