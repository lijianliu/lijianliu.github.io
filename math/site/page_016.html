💡 Set up two equal ratios with the SAME units on top: a/b = x/D. Then cross-multiply.

📚 Key words

Ratio
A comparison of two quantities, like 10 hops : 12 feet.
Proportion
Two equal ratios: 10/12 = x/90.
Cross-multiply
12x = 10 · 90 → x = 75.
Unit rate
Amount for 1 unit: 12 ÷ 10 = 1.2 feet per hop.
