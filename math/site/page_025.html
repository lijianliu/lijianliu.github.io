💡 x + 0.15x = 1.15x. Price + 15% tax/tip = 1.15 × price. Price − 20% off = 0.80 × price.

📚 Key words

Increase by p%
Multiply by (1 + p/100): +15% → 1.15x.
Decrease by p%
Multiply by (1 − p/100): −20% → 0.80x.
Original price
Divide the final amount by the multiplier.
Combine
x + 0.15x: x is 1x, so 1x + 0.15x = 1.15x.
