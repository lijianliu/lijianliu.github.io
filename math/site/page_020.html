💡 slope = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Keep the same order on top and bottom!

📚 Key words

Slope formula
m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁)
Table
Look at how y changes when x changes.
Common mistake
Putting x on top: that gives run/rise, the wrong answer.
Negative slope
y goes down as x goes up.

✏️ From the homework
