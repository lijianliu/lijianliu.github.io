19. Comparing two lines: who is faster?

D – Rates, proportions & slope • 10 questions

💡 Steeper line = faster rate. Compare the slopes, not where the lines start.

📚 Key words

Steeper
Bigger slope → grows faster.
Intersection
Where two lines cross, both have the same y at the same x.
Difference in rates
Subtract the slopes: 30 − 20 = $10 more per month.
