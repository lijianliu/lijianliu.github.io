11. Equation of a line from two points

C – Line of best fit • 10 questions
Topics← Prev11 / 30Next →
💡 slope m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Then b = y − m·x. Write y = mx + b.

📚 Key words

Slope (m)
Rise over run: change in y ÷ change in x.
y-intercept (b)
Where the line crosses the y-axis (x = 0).
Slope-intercept form
y = mx + b
Line of best fit
A line drawn through the middle of scatterplot data to show its trend.

✏️ From the homework

Through (2, 9) and (6, 17): m = (17−9)/(6−2) = 8/4 = 2; b = 9 − 2·2 = 5 → y = 2x + 5.
