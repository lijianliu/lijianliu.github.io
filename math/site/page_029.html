💡 Replace y in the second equation with what y equals in the first. Solve for x, then find y.

📚 Key words

Substitution
Swap a variable for an equal expression.
Parentheses
2x + 3(x − 1): keep the parentheses when you substitute!
Back-substitute
Plug the x you found into the easier equation to find y.

✏️ From the homework

y = x − 1 and 2x + 3y = 12 → 2x + 3(x − 1) = 12 → 5x = 15 → x = 3, y = 2 → (3, 2).
