💡 Line of best fit: y = −0.7x + 35.9 (x = car age in years, y = miles per gallon).

📚 Key words

Predict
Substitute the car age for x.
Solve backwards
Given mpg, set −0.7x + 35.9 = mpg and solve for x.
Slope −0.7
Each year older → 0.7 fewer mpg.
Intercept 35.9
A brand-new car gets about 35.9 mpg.

✏️ From the homework

Predict a 7-year-old car: y = −0.7(7) + 35.9 = 31 mpg.
