2. Independent & dependent variables

💡 Independent = the cause (x-axis, the one you change). Dependent = the result (y-axis, it "depends" on x).

📚 Key words

Independent variable
The input or cause. Goes on the x-axis (horizontal).
Dependent variable
The output or result. Its value depends on the independent variable. Goes on the y-axis.
Association
A relationship: when one variable changes, the other tends to change too.
Trick question?
Ask yourself: "Does A change B, or does B change A?" The one doing the changing is independent.

A2-01
A host plans how much pizza to order. Which is the independent variable?
✔A.number of people at a party
✘B.number of pizzas needed
✘C.party theme
✘D.day of the party
💡 The number of people at a party affects the number of pizzas needed, so number of people at a party is independent (x) and number of pizzas needed is dependent (y).
