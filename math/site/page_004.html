4. Is there an association? (compare the percents)

A – Two-way tables & association • 10 questions
Topics← Prev4 / 30Next →
💡 Compare the ROW PERCENTS, not the raw counts. Big difference → association. About the same → no association.

📚 Key words

Association (categorical)
The percent with a trait is clearly different between the groups.
No association
The percents are close (for example 1.05% vs 1.04%).
Why percents?
Groups have different sizes, so raw counts can fool you.

HW 7-110b: 1.05% vs 1.04% of buyers chose highly fuel-efficient trucks → almost the same → no association between fuel prices and buying them.

✘C.No, because the bigger group always wins.
✘D.No, because you can never tell from percents.
💡 The difference is 30 percentage points: large → association.
