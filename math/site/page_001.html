1. Completing two-way tables (row & column totals)

💡 Row total = add across. Column total = add down. Missing cell = total − the other cell.

📚 Key words

Two-way table
A table that sorts data by two categories at once (one along the rows, one along the columns).
Row total
Add every cell across a row.
Column total
Add every cell down a column.
Grand total
Bottom-right corner: the total of everything. Row totals and column totals both add up to it.
Missing cell
Subtract: total − the known cell.
