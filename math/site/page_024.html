24. Fraction Busters (clearing fractions)

E – Solving equations (Checkpoint 7) • 10 questions
Topics← Prev24 / 30Next →
💡 Multiply EVERY term on both sides by the least common multiple (LCM) of the denominators. The fractions disappear!

📚 Key words

Fraction Busters
Multiply both sides by a number that cancels every denominator.
LCM
Smallest number all denominators divide into.
Every term
Don’t forget whole numbers: they get multiplied too!
Check
Substitute the answer into the original equation.
