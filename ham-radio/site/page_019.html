19. The ionosphere & HF propagation

T3 Radio Wave Propagation • 4 questions
Topics← Prev19 / 50Next →
💡 The ionosphere reflects HF. More sunspots = better 10 m and 6 m, from dawn until just after sunset.

📚 Key words

Ionosphere
Charged layers of the upper atmosphere that can bounce HF waves back to Earth.
Propagation
How a radio signal travels from one place to another.
F region
The highest ionosphere layer. It gives the longest-distance bounces.
Sunspots
Dark spots on the Sun. More sunspots = stronger ionosphere = 10 m and 6 m open for long distance.
Sunspot cycle
Sunspots rise and fall about every 11 years.
Dawn to after sunset
Best time for long-distance 10-meter contacts (the Sun powers the F region).
