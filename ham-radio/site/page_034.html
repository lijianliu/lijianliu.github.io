📚 Key words

Schematic
A circuit drawing using standard symbols. It shows how parts connect.
Resistor
Zig-zag symbol. Limits current.
Transistor
Circle with three leads. Here it controls the flow of current to the lamp.
Lamp
Circle with an X. Lights up.
Battery
Long and short lines. Supplies power.
