23. Front-panel controls: VFO, scan & RIT

T4 Amateur Radio Practices • 4 questions
Topics← Prev23 / 50Next →
💡 Keypad or VFO sets the frequency. RIT fixes SSB voices that sound too high or too low.

📚 Key words

VFO
Variable Frequency Oscillator, the tuning knob that sets your frequency. Or type it on the keypad.
Scan
The radio steps through frequencies looking for activity.
SSB
Single sideband, a voice mode. If the voice sounds too high or low, adjust RIT.
RIT / Clarifier
Receiver Incremental Tuning: fine-tunes only what you hear.
Off frequency (FM)
Tuned slightly wrong. FM audio becomes distorted.
