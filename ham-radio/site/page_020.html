💡 Meteor = 6 m. Aurora = raspy. Sporadic E = strong 10/6/2 m. Ducting = temperature inversion, ~300 mi.

📚 Key words

DX
Long-distance contact.
Meteor scatter
Bouncing signals off the glowing trails of meteors. Best on 6 meters.
Auroral backscatter
Bouncing off the northern lights. Signals sound raspy and distorted.
Sporadic E
Surprise patches in the E layer that give strong signals on 10, 6 and 2 m.
Tropospheric ducting
Signals trapped in an air "tunnel", reaching about 300 miles on VHF/UHF.
Temperature inversion
Warm air sitting on top of cool air. It creates the duct.
