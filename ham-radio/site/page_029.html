29. Calculating power: P = I × E

T5 Electrical Principles • 4 questions
Topics← Prev29 / 50Next →
💡 Power (watts) = current (amps) × voltage (volts). Cover the letter you want in the triangle.

📚 Key words

P
Power in watts (W).
I
Current in amperes (A).
E
Voltage in volts (V).
P = I × E
Power = current × voltage. 13.8 V × 10 A = 138 W.
I = P ÷ E
Current = power ÷ voltage. 120 W ÷ 12 V = 10 A.
DC
Direct current, one direction, like a battery.
