41. Meters & soldering

T7 Practical Circuits • 11 questions
Topics← Prev41 / 50Next →
💡 Voltmeter in parallel, ammeter in series. Never use acid-core solder. Cold joint = lumpy.

📚 Key words

Voltmeter
Measures voltage (electric potential). Connect it in parallel (across the part).
Ammeter
Measures current. Connect it in series (in line with the part).
Multimeter
One meter for voltage, resistance and more. Measuring voltage on the resistance setting can damage it.
Ohmmeter
Measures resistance by sending a small current and reading the voltage. The circuit must be OFF.
Discharged capacitor
On an ohmmeter its resistance reading rises over time as it charges.
Solder
Melted metal that joins wires. Never use acid-core solder for electronics.
Cold joint
A bad solder joint. Rough or lumpy surface.
